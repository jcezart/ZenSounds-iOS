# Rosary content sources

Verified on 2026-10-13.

- The Portuguese prayers use the traditional Brazilian wording supplied in the ZenSounds specification (Appendix A), checked against the *Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church*, appendix “Common Prayers”: https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/pt/motu_proprio/documents/hf_ben-xvi_motu-proprio_20050628_compendio-catechismo.html
- The English prayers are traditional public-domain forms supplied in Appendix A. In particular, the Apostles’ Creed is **not** the copyrighted ICEL 2010 translation.
- The four sets of mysteries, the Luminous Mysteries, and the weekly schedule follow John Paul II, *Rosarium Virginis Mariae* (2002), especially no. 38: https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/pt/apost_letters/2002/documents/hf_jp-ii_apl_20021016_rosarium-virginis-mariae.html
- Scripture references are those listed in Appendix B of the feature specification. The Assumption and Coronation intentionally have no direct scripture reference.
- All short mystery meditations in `rosary_mysteries.json` are original ZenSounds text, written for this app and not copied from a book or website.

The prayer text is stored once per prayer in `rosary_prayers.json`; the 20 mysteries and their structured Bible references are stored in `rosary_mysteries.json`.
